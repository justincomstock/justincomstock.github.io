Privacy policy

openclaw-life · last updated September 27, 2026

openclaw-life is a personal assistant that Justin Comstock runs for himself. Only Justin's own accounts are connected to it.

What it accesses

With Justin's permission, it reads his Gmail messages and Google Calendar events, and, where he has approved it, creates drafts, labels and calendar holds for him. Nothing is sent from his accounts without his explicit approval.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to provide Justin's assistant features: summaries, reminders, search and help with his own tasks.
  • It is stored on a private server that Justin controls, reachable only from his own devices.
  • It is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising.
  • Justin can disconnect access at any time from his Google Account (Security → Third-party access), and can delete the stored data from his server.

Google API Services

openclaw-life's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
